Who it's for

Parents looking for support for primary or secondary learners, sixth-form and college students preparing for exams, and adult learners building new skills. Whatever your starting point, the first step is the same: a short, structured enquiry.

How it works

  1. Enquire — complete our short prototype enquiry form (level, subject, goals, availability, and how you'd like us to reply).
  2. Intro call — we reply within two working days to arrange a short introductory call.
  3. Trial lesson — meet your tutor and try a session before committing.
  4. Placement — we match you to the right tier and schedule.

Tutoring options

1-to-1 tutoring

Personalised sessions with flexible scheduling, priced per hour. Best for targeted support or confidence building.

Small-group classes

Fixed weekly schedule with a small group at a mid-range price. Best for steady progress alongside peers.

Exam-preparation intensives

Short, fixed courses focused on upcoming exams, priced per course. Best for a final structured push.
