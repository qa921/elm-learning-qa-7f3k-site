How we redesigned the tutoring enquiry

A concept page for Elm Learning QA-7f3k, based on a review of how comparable tutoring services handle enquiries.

The current enquiry journey

In the typical tutoring-service journey we reviewed, an enquirer lands on a homepage, digs through a services page, finds a generic "Contact" link — often in the footer — and is asked to write a free-text message with no guidance. The reply, if it comes, arrives days later with no stated next step.

What already works

Services that state subjects, levels, and a clear call-to-action on the homepage are easier to understand within seconds. Services that list their tiers up front let enquirers self-select before writing anything, which leads to clearer, more useful first messages.

What needs improvement

Findability: enquiry entry points are often buried behind vague "Contact" labels, adding unnecessary clicks.

Request burden & transparency: generic contact forms push the work onto the enquirer, who must guess what information to include; pricing and tier information is frequently absent.

Next-step clarity: most journeys give no response-time expectation and no description of what happens after submitting.

How the improved concept helps

Our concept puts a single, clearly labelled enquiry action on the homepage and keeps the ask small: learner level, subject, goals, availability, and a preferred reply channel — with contact details collected only for the channel chosen. In return, the enquirer gets a stated response time (within two working days) and a visible, named next step: an introductory call, then a trial lesson, then placement into the right tier. Less effort to enquire, more certainty about what happens next.
